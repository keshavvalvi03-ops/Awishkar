System Architecture & Research Methodology

Formal specification of the 6-layer explainable AI framework

Avishkar Research Submission

Project: Explainable AI-Based Clause Risk Analyser in Contract
Motivation: Existing legal tech literature (e.g. Hendrycks et al., Dikmen et al., Anusha & Rani, Sandhya B S) primarily scores individual clauses in isolation, ignores missing protective clauses, lacks confidence calibration, and uses black-box models without exact mathematical justification. This architecture directly addresses each of these gaps.

The Six Architectural Layers

Layer 1 — Clause Extraction (CUAD / Heuristic)
Extracts target spans and annotator answers using fine-tuned transformer QA models (RoBERTa/DeBERTa on CUAD) or calibrated word-bounded regex pattern matching with verified context windows.
Layer 2 — Risk Classification Taxonomy
Maps 41 CUAD categories and 40 extended commercial categories into business risk types (Financial, Restrictive Covenant, Termination, Renewal, IP, Operational, Legal/Compliance). Reuses existing categories without duplication.
Layer 3 — Rule-Based Risk Scoring
Calculates clause risk: Score = (Base Severity + Σ Keyword Modifiers) × Confidence + 5 × (1 - Confidence). Low-confidence extractions are pulled toward neutral (5/10) to prevent false alarms.
Layer 4 — Explainability & Exact Attribution
Generates natural language justifications and inspectable feature-attribution breakdowns. Unlike SHAP/LIME approximations, every decimal point in the score has an exact, auditable cause.
Layer 5 — Contract-Specific Missing Clause Detection
Applies customized checklists for 10 contract types. Classifies clauses as Expected, Conditional, or Optional, and reports status as Detected, Not detected — verify manually, or Uncertain.
Layer 6 — Cross-Clause Interactions & Aggregation
Detects compound risk traps (Uncapped Financial Exposure, Exclusive Lock-In Trap, Abrupt Walkaway Risk) and computes a confidence-calibrated contract-level score [0 - 10].
